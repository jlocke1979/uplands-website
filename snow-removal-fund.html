Why neighbors give

Clear walks are a shared benefit.

01

Safer winter walking

The fund is meant to help children, older neighbors, dog walkers, students, and visitors stay out of the street when a clearing plan is in place.

02

Who plows

Wilson Services plows this year. They come out for 2 or more inches and try to plow after the city clears the streets, though that timing is not always possible. Which sidewalks are included is not listed on this page.

03

Ready before the storm

Gifts ahead of winter help the association prepare before snow arrives.
